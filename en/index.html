The foundation was established at the end of 1984, ahead of the 600th anniversary of Ruperto Carola, with the aim of promoting science and scholarship. It was initiated by the then Rector Prof. Dr. Gisbert Freiherr zu Putlitz, the former chairman of the Volkswagen board Kurt Lotz, and the constitutional and tax law scholar Prof. Dr. Paul Kirchhof.
