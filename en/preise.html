Since 1990 the foundation has awarded the Ruprecht-Karls Prizes. They honour young researchers at Heidelberg University who have achieved outstanding research results. As a rule, the five best doctoral theses of the year are recognised. The multi-stage, university-wide selection process considers first scholarly works from all disciplines.

The decision is made by a jury appointed by the foundation’s Board on the Rector’s proposal. The jury, chaired by Prof. Dr. Rohini Kuner, decides on the basis of nominations from the faculties. Individual applications are not possible.

Award ceremony 2025

On 24 October 2025 the prizes, worth €3,000 each, were presented in the Alte Aula of the university. The winners:

  • Dr. Nicole Sara Colaianni (history)
  • Dr. Julia Lemp (global health)
  • Dr. Letitia Parcalabescu (computational linguistics)
  • Dr. Felix Draxler (computer science and mathematics)
  • Dr. Lukas Maximilian Sigmund (chemistry)

Fritz Grunebaum Prize: Dr. Sina Allgeier (law)

Prize winners’ reunion

After the first meeting in November 2019, former prize winners came together again in Heidelberg from 25 to 27 October 2024 – with the award ceremony, short talks by earlier winners, a trip to the observatory and plenty of networking, under the motto “Auf ein Wiedersehen!” (“See you again!”).

Since 1990, more than 190 people have been honoured (source: Heidelberg University press release, 20 October 2025).
