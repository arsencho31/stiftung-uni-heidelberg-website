Many university projects depend on financial support. In recent years the foundation has championed the following larger projects:

1st Heidelberg Foundation Symposium (2018)

With the symposium the foundation wanted to stimulate and promote international, interdisciplinary academic exchange on socially relevant topics. It took place at the International Science Forum Heidelberg – in the heart of the Old Town, an attractive setting for exchange between Heidelberg scholars and their guests from abroad.

New courtyard for the “Neue Universität”

For the university’s 625th birthday there was a gift: the foundation had collected donations for the modernisation of the “Neue Universität”, which made it possible to include the green courtyard of the lecture-hall building in the modernisation concept. The aim was to make the courtyard more visible as an outdoor space and more attractive as a place to linger. Trustees, board and friends of the foundation raised €250,000 for this project.

After a nearly two-year conversion, the building and its courtyard were ceremonially reopened on 25 June 2011. University commemorative publication (PDF, German)
