Gesellschaft der Freunde Universität Heidelberg e.V. →

The foundation awards prizes to young researchers and funds measures that improve, on site in Heidelberg, the conditions for lively academic exchange among university members and with guests from foreign universities. It also launches fundraising campaigns to help the university with grants for larger projects.

Kant 2024
For the 300th birthday of Immanuel Kant, the foundation supported a seminar series with the philosopher Omri Boehm on the spirit of the Enlightenment in January 2024 and invited students and staff to visit the Bonn exhibition “Kant und die offenen Fragen” together in March 2024.

Ruprecht-Karls Prizes
With the Ruprecht-Karls Prizes the foundation highlights the achievements of talented young researchers and motivates them to continue their academic careers. The prizes are awarded for outstanding scholarly work from all fields.

“UniHDhilft” campaign
Prompted by Putin’s war, the foundation – together with colleagues from the university’s service units and academic institutes – supports students, prospective students and early-career researchers who had to flee Ukraine and are now seeking a new academic home in Heidelberg.

Donate & get involved →

Has the war in Ukraine disrupted your studies or research? UniHDhilft supports students and junior researchers from Ukraine who want to continue their work or studies at Heidelberg University. Contact us at freunde@uni-heidelberg.de.
