The Kuratorium is elected for a term of five years and consists of people who have shown particular commitment to the foundation’s purpose. The members listed here were elected in autumn 2021. We were saddened by the loss of our highly valued trustee Dr. Jobst Wellensiek, who passed away in November 2022.
