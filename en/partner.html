Gesellschaft der Freunde Universität Heidelberg e.V. →

In May 2000 the foundation concluded a cooperation agreement with the Gesellschaft der Freunde Universität Heidelberg e.V. One aim of this cooperation is to pursue shared goals – such as promoting research and teaching at Heidelberg University – even more effectively.

In 2003 the foundation and the association legally consolidated their activities as far as permitted. Thanks to largely identical boards and a shared office, the two institutions can since then “appear and act as one” (preamble to the association’s statutes).

Partners in every respect

At regular intervals the affairs of the foundation and the association are discussed in joint board meetings.
