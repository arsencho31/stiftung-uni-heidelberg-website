Heidelberg University Foundation →

With about 1,400 members the Gesellschaft der Freunde is the largest association active at Ruprecht-Karls-Universität. More than three quarters of our members are alumni – people who have studied, taught, researched or worked in another capacity at Heidelberg University for a time.

As a member you support the alumni and funding work of our association. You get preferred access to our events and are regularly informed about what is happening at the university. In the general assembly, which must be convened at least once a year under the statutes, your vote counts in all resolutions.
