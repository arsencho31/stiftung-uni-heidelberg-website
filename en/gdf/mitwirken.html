We are grateful for the personal and financial commitment with which our members contribute to promoting and developing Ruperto Carola. Students and alumni at home and abroad, Heidelberg citizens and companies support the society’s goals as advisers, ambassadors, cooperation partners or donors. Your time, ideas, knowledge, experience and connections are of great value to us. A few examples:

Student involvement
For many years students have worked on the “Preis der Freunde”: they take care of the call for applications, review submissions and, as student jurors, prepare the selection. At the ceremony one of them gives the laudatory speech for the winning project group.

Active alumni
Former students, staff and scholars such as the film director Gero von Boehm or the writer Peter Bieri enrich our events as speakers, discussion partners, moderators or multipliers.

Companies
help us with know-how, professional services and donations: from the association’s bookkeeping, annual reports and legal advice to printing costs – and the challenge trophy “Preis der Freunde”, made in a company’s training workshop.

Would you like to get involved too? Then talk to us – we welcome your ideas and your interest: freunde@uni-heidelberg.de
