Heidelberg University Foundation →

Many students, graduates, staff and friends of Ruperto Carola want to stay in direct, long-term contact with the subject, institute or faculty they feel they belong to. That is why the statutes allow sections to be founded at faculty and institute level. There are seven so far; here is a selection:

Alumni Psychologici

The oldest section, founded by Prof. Joachim Funke and Dr. Herbert Wettig. Section head: Prof. Dr. Tanja Bipp, Institute of Psychology.

Physics and Astronomy

Founded by the former dean Prof. Dr. Christof Wetterich. Section head: the current dean of the Faculty of Physics and Astronomy.

Alumni, Friends and Supporters of Law

Created from an initiative of association members who teach at the Faculty of Law.

Alumni Economics

Started at the Alfred Weber Institute by Prof. Günter Liesegang. Section head: Barbara Neef, faculty officer at the Faculty of Economics and Social Sciences.

Members can join a section at any time.
