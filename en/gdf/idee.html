Heidelberg University Foundation →

Together with its partners, the Gesellschaft der Freunde Universität Heidelberg e.V. aims to build a network that is robust in several respects: it connects alumni with one another and with their university, promotes the exchange of experience between the university and its friends in business and culture, and pools ideal and material commitment to university projects and concerns that could not be realised without outside support.

The association was founded in 2003. It is a non-profit registered association (Registergericht Heidelberg) with subdivisions (sections) at faculty and institute level.

The statutes are the written constitution of our association. They name, among other things, the association’s goals and the tasks of the general assembly, and their preamble explains the legal predecessors of the society.

Read the statutes (PDF, German)

The society emerged in 2003 from the merger of the Verein der Freunde der Universität Heidelberg e.V. and the Universitäts-Gesellschaft Heidelberg e.V.
