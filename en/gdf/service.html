Events

Whether talks, alumni meetings, panel discussions, workshops or a museum visit under expert guidance: our events build bridges between business, culture and science. Together with our partners we regularly invite you to discover Heidelberg University and its worldwide network.

Information

We send print media to all members:

  • “Ruperto Carola” – the university’s research magazine, three times a year, with topics from the humanities, social sciences, natural sciences and medicine.
  • “HAIlife” – the alumni magazine, usually once a year, about alumni networks and activities as well as research, teaching and city life.
