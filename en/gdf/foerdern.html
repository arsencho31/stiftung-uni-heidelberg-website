Heidelberg University Foundation →

Every university needs friends who support it in fulfilling its tasks in research and teaching. With the help of its members, the society champions projects that arise at Ruprecht-Karls-Universität and benefit its students, teachers and institutions.

Our funding activities focus on supporting students and student initiatives, interdisciplinary projects of central institutions, and expanding alumni work.

Exceptional, interdisciplinary projects

These include the digitisation workshop, with which Germany’s oldest university library can not only preserve its cultural heritage of early prints and manuscripts but also make it accessible to scholars and the public. The book table, scanner and camera were financed from the association’s funds.

Students and student initiatives

Among the initiatives we support regularly is Nightline Heidelberg, a listening phone by students for students. Individual help such as grants towards the living costs of students during their exam phase is also part of this.

Expanding alumni work

Above all with our subject-based sections we support encounters between students and graduates, alumni days, graduation ceremonies and faculty days. The association also invests in the technical foundations of better, digital networking within Heidelberg Alumni International.
