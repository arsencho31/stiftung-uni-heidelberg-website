As a non-profit supporters’ association we have shaped and supported dialogue and exchange for many years with everyone who feels connected to Ruprecht-Karls-Universität Heidelberg and the city that has been its home for over 625 years. We work hand in hand – and since 2010 also door to door – with Heidelberg Alumni International, the worldwide alumni network of Ruperto Carola.

Do you, too, have a heart for Heidelberg University? However you feel connected to the old but lively lady Ruperto Carola – through your studies, your doctorate, your work or simply your love of the city – a membership of the Gesellschaft der Freunde Universität Heidelberg e.V. lets you give that connection visible expression.
