The activities of the Gesellschaft der Freunde are as varied as its tasks. A short overview.

Events
Encounters need occasions. So that our members stay in conversation with one another and with the university, we organise talks, alumni meetings, readings and excursions under expert guidance. And once a year, in summer, we invite everyone to the Tag der Freunde (Friends’ Day).

Sections
In our seven sections, section heads – mostly active Heidelberg professors and alumni – develop subject-specific contact and information offers. More about the sections →

Funding
“No money, no fun.” We look for – and have often found – generous supporters and partners who help us to help: from alumni work to the digitisation workshop of the university library. More →

Prizes
Good ideas and special achievements by student groups, individual students and young researchers deserve recognition – with the Preis der Freunde as well as individual grants and subsidies for student projects.
