Members of the Gesellschaft der Freunde are regularly invited to events organised by the association, partly together with its partners. A fixed date in the annual calendar is the Tag der Freunde (Friends’ Day): every year on a Friday in late June or early July.

Tag der Freunde 2026

On 24 July 2026 the friends of the university met at the International Science Forum Heidelberg in the Old Town.

4:00 pm – General assembly
about 5:15 pm – Presentation of the Preis der Freunde, followed by drinks on the terrace
from 6:00 pm – Walk together to the university’s summer party in the Marstallhof

Tag der Freunde 2025

On Friday, 4 July 2025, the society invited members to the Hotel Königstuhl: general assembly (4:30 pm), a talk “New gene therapies are revolutionising cancer medicine” by Prof. Dr. Carsten Müller-Tidow, Medical Director of the Department of Haematology, Oncology and Rheumatology (6:15 pm), the Preis der Freunde ceremony (7:15 pm) and a summer party with a view over Heidelberg and the Neckar.

New Year’s reception

For some years the society has invited its members in January to a lecture followed by a reception. On 10 January 2025 Prof. Dr. Anne Peters of the Max Planck Institute for Comparative Public Law and International Law spoke on “Brave new world order? International law in transition”.

Universitätsfreunde unterwegs (University friends on tour)

Themed excursions to places where influential current or former members of Heidelberg University live or work, and to places, institutions and companies that play an important role in business, culture, politics, art or science.

  • Europe in Strasbourg, May 2018
  • The old new Potsdam, April 2017 – including the GFZ research centre in the Albert Einstein Science Park and the Palais Barberini
  • Paris – experiencing history, April 2016
  • The Deutsche Bundesbank – more than just a bank, October 2015
  • Babylon in Berlin, April 2015
