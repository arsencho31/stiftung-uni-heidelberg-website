Members can be natural and legal persons as well as partnerships with legal capacity (§ 3 para. 1 of the statutes, in German). Members pay annual fees; the minimum fee is set by the general assembly, beyond that the amount is left to the member’s own assessment (§ 4 para. 1).

Annual fees

Following the general assembly’s resolution of 30 January 2004, the following apply since 2004:

Individuals€50.00 minimum per year
Companies / legal entities€250.00 minimum per year
Students€10.00 – for the duration of studies up to the first academic degree
Graduates€25.00 – for at most three years after the first academic degree

How it works

Membership is established by a declaration of accession to the Board and its acceptance by the Board (§ 3 para. 2 of the statutes). Download the form, fill it in and send it to us signed.
