The Gesellschaft der Freunde publishes the long-standing series Heidelberger Jahrbücher. It was founded in 1807 by Heidelberg professors as Heidelbergische Jahrbücher der Literatur and has an eventful history, interrupted by two world wars.

Since 2016 the yearbooks have appeared as “Heidelberger Jahrbücher Online” with Heidelberg University Publishing (heiUP): electronic, open access and, on request, as print on demand. The first online volume is titled Stabilität im Wandel.

Portrait volume “Universität Heidelberg. Menschen, Lebenswege, Forschung”
In the last printed volume 55, almost 100 active Heidelberg scholars introduce themselves in short, personal portraits. Available for €18.70 in bookshops or at the office; members receive a copy free of charge on request.
