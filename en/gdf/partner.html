“Coming together is a beginning, staying together is progress, and working together is success.”

Henry Ford

The Gesellschaft der Freunde Universität Heidelberg e.V. is the legal successor of two long-established associations that had already concluded a cooperation agreement with the Stiftung Universität Heidelberg in May 2000. One aim of this agreement, still in force today, is to pursue shared goals such as promoting research and teaching even more effectively.

In 2003 the society and the foundation legally consolidated their activities as far as permitted. Thanks to largely identical boards and a shared office they can since then “appear and act as one” (preamble to the statutes).

Another partner of our association is Heidelberg Alumni International. Together we work to expand the existing alumni network and turn loose ties between alumni and the university into firm, lasting and positive relationships – so that alumni become friends.
