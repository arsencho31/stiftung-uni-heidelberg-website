The UniHDhilft campaign is an initiative of staff members who see the need for support of Ukrainian students and early-career researchers in their daily work. The team comes from several institutes (including Slavic studies, East European history, sociology, psychology, theology, finance and tax law), service units such as the Studierendenwerk Heidelberg, as well as the foundation and the Gesellschaft der Freunde.

The team includes

Dr. Marlene Bainczyk-Crescentini, Dr. Stefan Beljean, Jonas Dinkelaker, Dr. Felicitas Fischer von Weikersthal, Prof. Dr. Joachim Funke, PD Dr. Thorsten Helm, Dr. Bettina Kaibach, PD Dr. Stefaniya Ptashnyk, Sabine zu Putlitz, PD Dr. Franziska Schedewie, Dr. Ivan Sablin, PD Dr. Karoline Thaidigsmann, Kristian Willenbacher

Mentorship programme

The UniHDhilft mentorship programme goes beyond financial support and helps refugees find their place in the Heidelberg community. Each scholarship holder is paired with a Heidelberg student or early-career researcher who helps with the first steps – enrolment, finding the lecture hall, setting up the canteen card or looking for housing. We hope lasting friendships will grow from it.

Would you like to help? For example as a mentor, or by donating your time? Get in touch: freunde@uni-heidelberg.de
