UniHDhilft – donations for refugee students and researchers

Prompted by the war in Ukraine, the foundation – together with colleagues and staff from the university’s service units and academic institutes – has launched a support campaign.

Many refugees from Ukraine have also arrived in Heidelberg, among them students, early-career researchers and researchers who are seeking – or have recently found – an academic home at Heidelberg University. The war has taken almost everything from them and their families; what remains is the hope of pursuing their studies and scientific ambitions in Heidelberg.

So that taking part in academic life succeeds quickly, a start-up programme is meant to remove financial hurdles. Donations are needed to pay for learning materials, language courses, tutorials, study and meeting spaces, or the translation of Ukrainian certificates and expert opinions. They are also meant to fund study and research grants lasting several months, bridging the time until secure, longer-term self-funding. Besides money, donations of time are very welcome.
